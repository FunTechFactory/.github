## Our apps

| App | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **[MisFincas](https://misfincas.com)** | Portfolio management for landlords in Spain: rents, expenses, contracts and the real net yield after taxes and deductions. | Released |
| **[KebabScore](https://kebabscore.com)** | Find kebab shops near you and rate them on meat, bread, sauces, value, cleanliness and speed. | Beta |
| **[Atlas](https://atlas.funtechfactory.com)** | A travel planner and trip log: trips day by day, with flights, trains, hotels and costs on a map, available offline. | In development |

## How we build

Small, useful apps on a boring, self-hosted stack: C#/.NET, TypeScript (React, SvelteKit), PostgreSQL and SQLite, running in Docker on our own servers, with a shared sign-in for all our apps.
